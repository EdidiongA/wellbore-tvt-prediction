# arXiv Submission. Steps and Metadata

1. Account: arxiv.org; register with your academic email; first-time submitters in a category may need an endorsement (the system will tell you and provide an endorsement code to share with any published arXiv author in the category).
2. Primary category: **physics.geo-ph** (Geophysics). Cross-list: **cs.LG** (Machine Learning).
3. Upload: the PDF (rogii_preprint_Anwanane.pdf) is accepted directly for non-TeX submissions.
4. License: recommended **CC BY 4.0** (maximizes citability; compatible with later journal submission to Applied Computing and Geosciences, which permits preprints).
5. Title: Blind-Zone TVT Prediction in Horizontal Wells: An Information-Ceiling Study with Deployment-Side Dose Calibration
6. Abstract: paste from the manuscript's abstract (first section).
7. After posting: add the arXiv ID to the ACG cover letter and to your NIW evidence file.
